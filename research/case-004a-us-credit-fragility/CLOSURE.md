# CASE-004A — US Credit / ABCP Fragility

## Status

HISTORICAL RECONSTRUCTION COMPLETE
PREDICTIVE VALIDATION NOT ESTABLISHED
RESEARCH PHASE CLOSED — AUDIT ITEMS OPEN

## Research Question

Can credit deterioration during ABCP expansion,
combined with the BIS credit-to-GDP gap,
identify transitions toward funding stress?

## Historical Findings

- BIS credit gap was elevated before the 2007 funding crisis.
- Mortgage delinquency deterioration coincided with ABCP expansion in 2006–2007.
- ABCP contraction and TED spread widening appeared in 2007Q3.
- V0 and V1 were compared over 88 quarters, 2003Q1–2024Q4.
- V1 improved alert continuity but did not detect new crises.
- Neither V0 nor V1 provided advance Stage 2/3 alerts
  for the 2020 COVID shock or 2023 regional-bank stress.

## Limitations

- The framework was designed after observing the 2008 crisis.
- No independent forecasting performance was established.
- Historical BIS vintages and complete release timing are unverified.
- Original ABCP historical-vintage verification remains incomplete.
- CPFF crisis-period missingness remains unexplained.
- Historical stage labels do not establish causation.
- The archived June 2006 Federal Reserve evidence
  supports only part of the contemporary-data reconstruction.

## Closure Decision

Close the exploratory historical-reconstruction phase.

Do not claim:
- Universal financial crisis prediction
- Validated early-warning accuracy
- Incremental predictive superiority over BIS

Freeze V0 and V1 as historical research candidates.

Open audit items are preserved for future review.

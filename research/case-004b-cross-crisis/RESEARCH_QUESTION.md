# CASE-004B — Cross-Crisis Financial Amplification

## Primary Research Question

What common financial-system vulnerabilities amplify
different shocks into systemic financial crises?

## Objective

Identify financial vulnerabilities that repeatedly precede
or amplify systemic banking crises across countries and time.

Do not assume one universal crisis trigger exists.

## Hypotheses

H1 — Credit Expansion:
Excessive private credit growth may increase vulnerability.

H2 — Funding Fragility:
Reliance on unstable or noncore funding may amplify shocks.

H3 — Loss Absorption:
Lower bank capital buffers may increase crisis vulnerability.

H4 — Resilience:
Countries with similar vulnerabilities may experience
different outcomes because of structural resilience factors.

## Research Design

1. Verify historical panel data availability.
2. Identify independently defined crisis episodes.
3. Compare pre-crisis and non-crisis country-years.
4. Examine credit, funding and bank-capital mechanisms.
5. Test across different countries and historical periods.
6. Compare against established BIS early-warning indicators.
7. Audit historical data availability and robustness.

## Evidence Rules

- Define hypotheses before inspecting outcomes.
- Do not calibrate thresholds to fit 2008.
- Distinguish crisis onset from crisis duration.
- Do not treat observations from one crisis as
  independent successful predictions.
- Do not confuse association with causation.
- Separate historical reconstruction from forecasting.
- Keep held-out countries and periods for validation.
- Record missing data without filling silently.
- Preserve source revisions and data provenance.

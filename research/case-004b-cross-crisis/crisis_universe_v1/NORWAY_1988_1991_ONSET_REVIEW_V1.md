## 3. OBSERVED in published official histories

- Banking distress affected smaller institutions
  during 1988-1990.
- Difficulties spread to major banks.
- By 1991 the banking crisis became systemic.
- Guarantee-fund resources were depleted.
- Public intervention and bank recapitalization
  became necessary.

These are historical accounts, not measurements
from a contemporaneously available forecasting dataset.

## 4. INFERRED

The JST 1988 and IMF 1991 dates plausibly refer
to different stages of one extended banking crisis.

This is a strong reconciliation hypothesis,
not yet a verified equivalence of dataset rules.

## 5. NOT ESTABLISHED

- Exact mapping of each database's onset rules.
- Institution-level funding transmission causality.
- Whether asset losses preceded funding pressure
  across institutions.
- Whether the sequence generalizes across countries.
- Whether the conditions predict crises out of sample.

## 7. Research implication

Distinguish:
- early institution-level distress;
- system-wide escalation;
- official systemic-crisis onset.

Do not automatically treat a date disagreement
as two independent crises.

Do not use this discovery case as a pristine
out-of-sample validation event.

Next: audit remaining discordant events and
reserve non-crisis controls before mechanism testing.

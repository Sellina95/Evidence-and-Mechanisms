# CASE-004B — Global Crisis Sampling Protocol V1

Status: INVENTORY BUILT / SAMPLING NOT FROZEN

## Objective

Identify common financial-system vulnerabilities and
conditional shock-transmission mechanisms across crises.

The target is an explanatory, falsifiable model,
not a universal crisis prediction formula.

## Universe

Initial layer:
JST R6 archived banking-crisis onset observations.

Next layer:
IMF Laeven-Valencia (2026), systemic banking crises,
1970-2025, including identified borderline episodes.

Do not concatenate datasets without reconciling
country identifiers, crisis definitions, and dates.

## Pilot cases

USA 2007
SWE 1991
JPN 1997

These cases have been examined already.
They cannot serve as independent validation cases.

## Sampling requirements

1. Inspect country and time-period coverage.
2. Review crisis definitions and historical onset dates.
3. Account for globally correlated crisis waves.
4. Separate case-discovery from model-validation samples.
5. Do not choose cases based on confirming a hypothesis.
6. Include non-crisis and stress-without-crisis cases.
7. Check data availability without selecting for success.
8. Represent different geographic and financial systems.
9. Reserve untouched cases before fitting a model.
10. Treat calendar clustering as a provisional grouping.

## Mechanism dimensions to assess

- Preexisting credit and balance-sheet vulnerabilities
- Initial documented stress
- Candidate triggering shock
- Asset losses and collateral pressure
- Liquidity or refinancing stress
- Counterparty and network transmission
- Amplification and policy intervention
- Crisis outcome or shock absorption

Do not assume every case follows every stage.

## Current limitations

JST covers 18 advanced economies.
Its crisis chronology is not a global population.
The archival event panel may omit source metadata.
Event independence is not established.
No causal transmission is proven.

## Next gate

Expand and reconcile the crisis universe using IMF.
Then finalize a stratified deep-dive sample and
a separate validation sample before further modeling.

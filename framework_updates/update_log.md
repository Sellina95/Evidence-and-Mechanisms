Rules
- Completing a case does not automatically require a framework update.
- Every update should trace back to an evidence-bearing case.
- Preserve uncertainty and conditionality.
- Do not convert an unresolved result into a reusable explanation.
- A framework may be strengthened, narrowed, conditioned, or rejected as new evidence accumulates.

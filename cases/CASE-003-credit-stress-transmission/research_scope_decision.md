# CASE-003 — Research Scope Decision

Date: 2026-10-21
Status: SCOPE DECIDED — EMPIRICAL PROTOCOL NOT FROZEN
Empirical results consulted: NONE

## Decision

The research will distinguish two related but different questions.

### Primary — Credit Stress Transmission

Does the CCC–BB OAS differential provide earlier and
incremental warning of subsequent broad credit-market
stress compared with aggregate HY OAS alone?

The primary outcome must represent subsequent credit
deterioration, not contemporaneous spread co-movement.

Because aggregate HY OAS includes BB and CCC-rated
constituents, mechanical overlap must be explicitly
evaluated.

The exact credit-stress outcome, forecast horizon,
threshold, and leakage controls remain unresolved.

### Secondary — Equity Market Early Warning

Does adding CCC–BB to aggregate HY OAS improve
out-of-sample prediction of broad equity-market stress?

Candidate outcome:
S&P 500 decline of at least 5% from the decision-date
close within the next 20 trading days.

This is a candidate, not a frozen specification.

## Required Comparison

Model A:
Aggregate HY OAS level and trailing change.

Model B:
Model A plus CCC–BB differential level and trailing change.

Evaluate both on identical dates with the same
information-availability assumptions.

Incremental out-of-sample performance, not
historical visual correlation, determines usefulness.

## Interpretation Rules

- Predictive association is not proof of causation.
- Earlier movement alone is not sufficient.
- Mechanical index overlap is not independent evidence.
- Failure to improve the baseline is a valid result.
- Equity-warning performance does not automatically
  establish credit-transmission causality.

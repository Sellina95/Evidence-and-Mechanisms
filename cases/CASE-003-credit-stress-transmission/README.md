# CASE-003 — Credit Stress Transmission & Early Warning

**Status: OPEN — Research design / data licensing pending**
**Research start: 2026-10-21**
**Production impact: NONE**

## 1. Observation

Credit stress may not develop uniformly across high-yield rating
categories. Lower-rated CCC & Below bonds may experience spread
widening relative to BB bonds before broad market stress emerges.

This is a research motivation, not an established observation.

## 2. Baseline Framework

The aggregate ICE BofA US High Yield OAS measures broad high-yield
credit conditions.

A widening CCC–BB differential may indicate disproportionate
deterioration in lower-quality credit.

Neither indicator is presumed superior before empirical testing.

Baseline status: DRAFT — not frozen.

## 3. Observed Deviation

UNKNOWN — no empirical test performed.

## 4. Research Question

Does the CCC & Below minus BB option-adjusted spread differential
provide earlier and more useful warning of broad market stress
than aggregate high-yield OAS alone?

Out of scope:
- Trading strategy optimization

## 5. Competing Hypotheses

### H1 — Incremental Early Warning

CCC–BB deterioration contains forward-looking stress information
beyond aggregate HY OAS.

Falsification:
No out-of-sample improvement over an aggregate HY OAS baseline.

Status: OPEN

### H2 — Redundant Credit Information

CCC–BB mainly reflects information already captured by aggregate
HY OAS.

Falsification:
Robust incremental out-of-sample predictive improvement.

Status: OPEN

### H3 — Composition / Liquidity Effects

Observed differential widening may reflect rating composition,
sector concentration, liquidity, or index methodology rather than
a generalizable market-stress warning.

Falsification:
Evidence of stable incremental performance across regimes and
after feasible composition/liquidity controls.

Status: OPEN

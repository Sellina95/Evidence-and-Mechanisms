# CASE-001 — Intraday and Sequence Reconstruction

## Objective

Determine when the post-FOMC reversal began and whether the sequence of market moves helps distinguish among H1, H2, and H3.

This timeline focuses on ordering rather than causal attribution.

---

## Event Sequence

| Phase | New Information / Market State | Rates / Curve | USD | Equities | Oil / External Factors | Hypothesis Implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-FOMC | A 25 bp hike was already heavily priced | Tightening expectations elevated | USD supported | Risk assets cautious | Oil above $100 and inflation pressure elevated | Supports H1 pre-pricing component |
| Sep 16, 2:00 p.m. ET | Fed raises target range 25 bp to 3.75–4.00%; SEP released | Initial repricing begins | USD strengthens | Equities weaken | Energy shock remains in background | Establishes policy-event anchor |
| Press conference / late session | Warsh emphasizes persistent inflation and price stability | 2Y rises materially more than long end; curve flattens | USD gains | Equity selloff intensifies | No clear external reversal yet | Weakens simple immediate sell-the-fact version of H1; directionally consistent with H2 |
| Sep 16 close | Hawkish interpretation remains dominant | 2Y +7 bp vs Sep 15; 10Y +1 bp; 30Y -1 bp | USD higher | S&P lower; Nasdaq approximately flat | Oil remains elevated | Clear maturity divergence established |
| Overnight / Sep 17 pre-U.S. session | Markets continue processing Fed communication | Short-end remains restrictive while long end stabilizes / finds relief | USD remains relatively firm initially | U.S. equity futures recover | External information continues to arrive | Important evidence that reversal begins before the Sep 17 U.S. cash close; increases relevance of H2 |
| Sep 17 U.S. session | Post-FOMC reversal broadens | Treasury yields decline | USD partially reverses | S&P and Nasdaq rally | Oil declines; short-covering and profit-taking also reported | H1, H2, and H3 all remain plausible contributors |
| Sep 18 | Risk recovery continues in several assets | Yields partially rebound | TBD | S&P and Nasdaq rise further | Oil declines further | Suggests the episode cannot be described as a one-period reaction |

---

## Sequence Finding

The September 2026 episode was not an immediate “hike priced in → sell the fact” reversal.

The hawkish market response strengthened through the Fed communication period on September 16. The subsequent divergence emerged later, with the long end of the Treasury curve finding relative relief and equity futures recovering before the September 17 U.S. cash session.

This sequence weakens a simple version of H1 in which positions were immediately unwound after the expected hike.

It is directionally consistent with H2 because short-term policy expectations remained restrictive while the long end showed relative relief.

However, H3 cannot be excluded because falling oil prices and other information also entered the market during the reversal period.

The current evidence therefore supports a multi-stage repricing rather than a single simultaneous cross-asset reaction.

---

## Identification Limit

Sequence alone does not establish causality.

The next step is to identify which observable variables can discriminate between:

1. position unwinding,
2. inflation-credibility / term-structure repricing,
3. and the reversal of the external energy shock.
